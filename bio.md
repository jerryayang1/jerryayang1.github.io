## About me

I am a process engineer focused on nanofabrication, plasma etch, thin films,
and metrology. My work connects experimental process development with the
careful characterization needed to make advanced fabrication repeatable.

## Areas of interest

- Plasma etch and high-aspect-ratio pattern transfer
- Thin-film deposition and process integration
- Metrology, experimentation, and data-driven optimization
