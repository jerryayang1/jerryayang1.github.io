## About me

Dr. Jerry A. Yang received his PhD in Electrical Engineering and Master's in
Education from Stanford University. His research interests include
heterogeneous integration of two-dimensional semiconductors into commercially
viable applications as well as educating the future semiconductor workforce.
At Stanford, he was an awardee of the National Science Foundation Graduate
Research Fellowship and the Achievement Rewards for College Scientists
Foundation Scholarship. Prior to Stanford, he received his BS in Electrical
and Computer Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin. In his free
time, he enjoys cats, cat cafes, getting only tea at coffee shops, boba,
pickleball, and trying niche hobbies.

## Areas of interest

- Heterogeneous integration of two-dimensional (2D) materials into devices
  and systems
- Unconventional nanofabrication techniques
- Semiconductor workforce development and engineering education
